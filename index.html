Scoring formulas

Complete: DS = 40. TS = max(0, 40 × (1 − |Runtime − TT| / TT)).

Incomplete: DS = 40 × (185 − distance from finish) / 185; TS = 0.

MS = (4R + 0.1N) × distance fraction. Half roll = R 0.5.

Ramp: MS = 0 and DS × 0.5.

Penalties: missed impound ×0.70; each construction violation ×0.80; each competition violation ×0.90.
